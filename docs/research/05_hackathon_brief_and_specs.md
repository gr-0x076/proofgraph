## Selected Problem Statement: ProofGraph
* **Track:** Advanced / Product
* **Problem Brief:** Build an autonomous AI investigation platform where specialized agents independently research information, verify factual claims, challenge unreliable findings, detect contradictions, and validate evidence against original sources. The platform constructs an interactive knowledge graph connecting claims, sources, supporting evidence, and conflicting findings to generate transparent research reports with traceable citations and uncertainty assessments.
* **Prerequisites:** Python, Large Language Models (LLMs), Multi-Agent Systems, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Knowledge Graphs, Web Search APIs, Graph Databases, Information Retrieval.
* **Roadmap Milestones:**
  1. Research Query Understanding
  2. Multi-Agent Architecture Design
  3. Autonomous Source Collection
  4. Claim Extraction Engine
  5. Evidence Retrieval and Verification
  6. Independent Agent Cross-Verification
  7. Contradiction Detection
  8. Source Credibility Assessment
  9. Knowledge Graph Construction
  10. Interactive Evidence Visualization
  11. Citation Validation
  12. Research Report Generation
